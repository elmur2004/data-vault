System · Interaction

Drag & drop states

Cards are draggable on every board. A drop never commits on its own — it opens the target stage's form, and cancelling puts the card back.

01 · REST
Youssef Amin
COLD CALL
1px hairline border, 1px shadow, cursor: grab.
02 · LIFT
Youssef Amin
COLD CALL
Border takes the source stage bar colour, −1.4° rotation, 1.02 scale, 30px shadow.
03 · VALID TARGET
WON 1
Well keeps its tint, gains a 1px stage border plus a 2px offset outline at 33% opacity.
04 · BLOCKED TARGET
WON 1
ADMIN-ONLY COLUMN
Danger-tinted well, no outline, persistent dashed note. Drop is refused and a toast explains why.
05 · BLOCKED MESSAGE
! Only the portal admin can move a deal to Won.
Pattern: Only {role} can {action}. Terminal columns read “Won and Lost are terminal — cards cannot be dragged back out.”
06 · DROP CONTRACT
  1. Card is dropped on a valid column.
  2. That stage's form opens in a modal — only its fields, nothing else.
  3. Required fields block confirm and are named in a toast.
  4. Confirm moves the card, writes a stage record and a history line.
  5. Cancel reverts — the card never left its column.
